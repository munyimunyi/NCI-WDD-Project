Clean Water and Sanitation - International Cooperation

Target 6.a (Internation Cooperation) aims to help developing countries get the money, technology and training they need to improve water and sanitation systems. It focuses on international cooperation and capacity-building for things like water harvesting, desalination (removing salt from water), water-use efficiency, wastewater treatment and recycling or re-use technologies.

Indicator 6.a.1 measures how much water and sanitation-related Official Developmenty Assistance (ODA) is included in national budgets. When this support is well-aligned with national budgets and priorities, it can help countries close financial gaps, plan long-term projects and reach people who still lack safe drinking water and basic sanitation services.

Key Facts on Goal 6.a

  • SDG 6.a aims to promote international cooperation so developing countries can improve water and sanitation systems.
  • Indicator 6.a.1 tracks how Official Development Assistance (ODA) is included in national spending plans.
  • This support covers a range of things like water harvesting, water-use efficiency and wastewater treatment, not just taps and toilets.
  • Better-targeted ODA helps countries close financial gaps and scale up safe, affordable services for people who left behind.
